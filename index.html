Frequently asked questions

Common questions about Monona Coach as your AI basketball shooting coach.

What is an AI basketball shooting coach?

An AI basketball shooting coach uses computer vision and machine learning to analyze your shooting form from video, compare it to professional players, and give personalized feedback on each phase of your shot.

How does Monona Coach analyze my shot?

Record your shot from a side view with your phone. Monona Coach tracks body keypoints, breaks your motion into phases like setup and release, and compares your form to NBA player models with similarity scores and coaching tips.

Is Monona Coach free?

Yes. Monona Coach is free to download on iOS. You can record shots, get AI analysis, pro comparisons, and personalized drills at no cost.

Can I compare my form to NBA players?

Yes. Monona Coach matches your shot to 20+ NBA players or lets you pick a model player. You get phase-by-phase comparison, similarity percentages, and visual replay side by side with pros.

What do I need to record my shot?

Use your iPhone to film from a side view with one shooter and one basketball in frame. A clear view of your full shooting motion from setup through follow-through gives the best results.
